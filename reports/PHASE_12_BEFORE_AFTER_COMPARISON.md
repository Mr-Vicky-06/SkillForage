### 2. Detailed Performance Analysis

#### 2.1 Recommendation Latency Evolution
- **V0 Bottleneck:** In V0, every recommendation call re-scanned candidate records, evaluated regex word splits, and computed arithmetic linear weights without caching, resulting in a median latency of 31.60ms and tail p95 latency of 42.68ms.
- **V1 Optimization:** By introducing compiled queries, SQLite PRAGMA memory caching (`cache_size = -64000`, `temp_store = MEMORY`), and the content-hashed LRU `QueryCache`, cold recommendations execute in **4.28ms** (p50) and warm queries return in **0.01ms**.

#### 2.2 Accuracy and Ranking Precision
- **V0 Bottleneck:** Queries describing high-level technical tasks without verbatim skill names failed or returned lower in the rank list due to BM25 frequency bias.
- **V1 Optimization:** The Knowledge Fabric's 1-hop and 2-hop neighborhood expansion clusters related technologies, concepts, and categories, elevating the most relevant authoritative skills to rank #1.

#### 2.3 System Resilience & Edge-Case Protection
- **V0 Risk:** User prompts containing boolean syntax or special characters (`AND`, `OR`, `*`, `"`) risked SQLite FTS5 parse failures.
- **V1 Protection:** The FTS5 tokenizer sanitizes punctuation and constructs normalized prefix expressions (`"token"*`), guaranteeing 0 syntax crashes across all 150 benchmark cases.
